The core idea

A blind station still tells you
when a car arrived and left.

That gap is work time plus time stuck waiting. Being stuck leaves a fingerprint — a blocked car departs the instant a space frees downstream. So every observation is either an exact measurement or a known upper bound. That is a censored-likelihood problem.
